Prohibited trading rules

We welcome every trading strategy except those designed to exploit our programme.

Our risk management framework strictly forbids latency arbitrage, high-frequency collusion, and exploiting data feed gaps. Traders must operate within fair market parameters to ensure transparency across all accounts.

Risk Management Basics

Mastering leverage and position sizing is key to surviving volatile market conditions.

Never risk more than 1-2% of your total account equity on a single trade. Implementing automated stop-loss orders and understanding daily drawdown limits protects your capital from unexpected market spikes.

Algorithmic Strategies

How automated models help remove emotional bias from your execution workflow.

Algorithmic trading allows rules-based execution that monitors multiple pairs simultaneously. By backtesting data effectively, you can validate an edge before deploying real capital into live feeds.
